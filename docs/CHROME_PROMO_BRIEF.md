계정은 사장님이 직접 만든다. 에이전트는 계정을 만들거나 비밀번호를 넣을 수 없다.

|---|---|---|
| 유튜브 | 새 채널 "불멍" (지금 쓰는 구글 계정에서 채널만 하나 더 만든다) | 쇼츠는 구독자가 0이어도 추천에 뜬다. 다른 주제 채널에 섞으면 두 채널 다 추천이 흐려진다. |

## 공통 규칙 (매 회차 함께 붙여 넣기)

```

I made a campfire you can leave running in a browser tab. You can also write a worry on paper and throw it in.
   링크: https://bulmeong.web.app/en/
   올린 뒤 내 글에 첫 댓글을 단다:
It plays crackling wood and rain, and the fire grows when you tap it. Write something on a sheet of paper, swipe it up, and it burns. What you write stays on your device. Other visitors only see that someone threw a sheet in.

There are 20 hidden words that change the flame, and the first person to find each one gets their nickname in a shared album. "Leave it on" hides every button and has a 30/60/90-minute sleep timer.

Free, no sign-up, no ads. English, Korean, Japanese, Chinese and Spanish. Happy to hear what breaks on your phone.

2) r/webdev 에 Showoff Saturday 글을 올린다. 토요일에만 되는 글이다. 미국 시간으로 토요일이 아니거나, 규칙에서 정한 말머리(flair)를 붙일 수 없으면 올리지 않는다.
   제목:
Showoff Saturday: a campfire for your browser tab, with the flame drawn by a WebGL shader
   본문:
https://bulmeong.web.app/en/

Static HTML and ES modules, no bundler. The flame is a fragment shader (fbm noise shaped into tongues of fire), rendered offscreen and drawn onto a 2D canvas. Without WebGL it falls back to plain canvas paths.

Shared state (the burn counter and who found each hidden word first) is one Lambda and one DynamoDB table. Text you type never leaves the device. Five languages, picked from the browser language.

I built it with Claude Code, and the art was made with Codex. Feedback on mobile performance is welcome.
```
